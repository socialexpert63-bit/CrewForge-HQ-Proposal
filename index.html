What I found

Thirteen issues, four that matter most

I read the homepage, How It Works, About Us, Privacy and Terms. I did not submit the contact form or open the trial link behind its login, so I will confirm those with you on day one.

Critical

Trial link points at a development build

The main button leads to a Vercel preview of the develop branch. Visitors may hit an error or a login, and a staging build with its share token is public.

Critical

Two offers at once

The top of the page offers a 14-day free trial. The bottom says early access, “Reserve Your Spot” and limited founding spots. Visitors cannot tell what they are signing up for.

Critical

SMS consent does not match the product

Your policies say people consent by ticking a box on a website form. The product texts callers who phoned and missed the call, and they never saw that form. This is a carrier-registration and legal risk for your counsel to review.

Critical

No way to contact the company

Privacy and Terms send people to “the contact information on our website”, but the site shows no email, phone, address or named person.

High

Hidden pricing, no proof, unexplained claims

No price or range, no testimonials, demo, team or example page. “Within seconds” and “works with your existing number” are never explained or shown.

High

Thin legal pages

No effective date, no legal entity in the body, nothing on data retention, deletion requests, refunds or cancellation.

Medium / Low

Housekeeping

Template text in “Our Philosophy”, duplicated sections, a 2025 copyright, one page title and description on every page, a “Jax 2.0” image label, and an inconsistent list of trades.

The plan

Four phases, approved one at a time

Each phase stands alone. Phase 1 can start the day you say yes.

1
Week 1

Quick fixes

  • Move the trial button to a production URL on your own domain (for example app.crewforgehq.com).
  • Revoke the exposed preview share link and turn on protection for preview deployments.
  • Settle the offer: a live free trial or a founding waitlist, worded the same on every page.
  • Add a contact page and footer details, and link them from Privacy and Terms.
  • Remove template text, duplicate sections and internal labels; update the copyright year.
Done when

The trial button opens a working page on your own domain in a private browser window. The old preview link no longer works. One offer appears on every page. The contact page is live and linked from Privacy and Terms.

You provide

Access to Framer, Vercel and the domain. Your decision: live trial or founding waitlist.

2
Weeks 2 to 3

Compliance and trust

  • Draft a consent flow that covers people texted after a missed call: opt-in on the contractor's side, a written contractor consent confirmation, identification and opt-out wording in the first message, and disclosure of automation.
  • Expand Privacy and Terms: dates, legal entity, retention, deletion, subprocessors, refunds and cancellation.
  • Prepare the pages for A2P 10DLC brand and campaign registration and support the submission with your SMS provider.
  • Add a plain section on how it works with an existing phone number.
Done when

The consent flow and policy pages are drafted and ready for your counsel. The registration pack is complete and submitted. The “works with your number” section is live.

You provide

SMS provider access, your counsel’s review, and the business details a carrier registration asks for.

I am a developer, not a lawyer. Your counsel should review all consent and policy wording before it goes live.

3
Weeks 3 to 6

Conversion and proof

  • A 60 to 90 second demo video and dashboard screenshots.
  • One live sample SEO service page as a showcase.
  • Pricing or a founding-member rate, an FAQ, and a team and story section.
  • Layout for two or three pilot customer results and quotes, once you have them.
Done when

The demo, sample page, pricing, FAQ and team section are live. At least one pilot result is in place, if you have one by then.

You provide

Your pricing decision, pilot results and quotes, and team photos and short bios.

4
Monthly from week 6 · optional

Search and growth

  • Unique titles and descriptions, image alt text, sitemap, Open Graph images, Search Console and analytics.
  • One landing page per trade: plumbing, HVAC, electrical, garage door.
  • Funnel tracking from visit to form to trial to paying customer, with a monthly report.
Done each month when

You receive a short report: traffic, trial starts, what I fixed and what I suggest next.

You provide

Approval of the landing-page topics and access to analytics.
